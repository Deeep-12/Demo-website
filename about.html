Our Story & Sustainable Roots

Organic Garden was founded on a simple realization: that modern urban lifestyles thrive when reconnected with the biological rhythms of growing plants.

The Seed of an Idea

Established in 2018 in a tiny sunlit greenhouse, Organic Garden grew out of a passion for high-germination heirloom crop seed banking. Over years of collaborating with traditional farmers, we cataloged organic non-hybridized flowers and crop strains that thrive in standard container settings, backyards, and windowsills.

Today, we ship to over 50,000 gardeners annually, providing certified non-GMO seeds, acclimated live house plants, and zero-plastic botanical essentials. We are not just a supply company; we are your full-cycle partner in cultivation.

How We Grew

A timeline of sustainable milestones and green milestones.

2018

The Sprout

Founded in a backyard potting shed with only 12 heirloom tomato and flower seed selections.

2020

Compostable Packaging

Phased out standard plastic poly-bags. Launched our trademark 100% biodegradable cornstarch paper seeds packets.

2023

Live Plants Nursery

Opened our first state-of-the-art solar-heated indoor nursery, delivering vibrant potted botanicals directly to homes.

2026

Community Gardens Initiative

Pledged 2% of all order profits to urban greening programs and public elementary school seedling workshops.
