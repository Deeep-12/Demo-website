Growing Answers

Quick resolutions to common concerns about seed propagation, plant wellness, and deliveries.

We stand behind a 90-day dynamic replacement guarantee. All seed lots are laboratory tested for a minimum of 85% germination. If you follow our custom sowing instructions and they fail to sprout, simply reach out to us and we'll ship out a replacement batch at zero cost to you.
Our live plants are secured using thermodynamic, plant-safe modular boxes that keep moisture inside the root mass while providing optimal airflow. We wrap the biodegradable terracotta pots in recycled cellulose sleeves to prevent any cracking or soil shifting.
Yes, 100% of our products are strictly non-hybrid, open-pollinated, non-GMO, and organic. We adhere strictly to safe agricultural seed pledges, banking only traditional wild flower types and natural heirloom garden varieties.
